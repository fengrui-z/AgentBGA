# P4 base Action-card status

Updated: 2026-08-25

P4 is complete for all five base-game Action cards on sides I and II. The
runtime locks the printed side and strength when an action starts, exposes only
legal resumable choices, moves the selected Action card after the action, and
resolves after-finishing effects only after that move.

## Delivered

- **Cards I/II:** the complete strength 1–5 draw/discard tables, two Break
  steps, Snap thresholds, deck/display sources, Reputation range, physical
  display gaps, mandatory discards, and delayed display refill.
- **Build I/II:** Map 0 polyhex placement, rotations, adjacency, costs, Build-II
  cells, different-building and shared-strength constraints, special
  enclosures, map rewards, and the immediate optional migration of eligible
  Animals from standard enclosures into a newly built Reptile House or Large
  Bird Aviary.
- **Animals I/II:** the complete strength 1–5 card limits, card-first selection,
  prerequisites, terrain and enclosure checks, partner-zoo discounts, special
  enclosures and Flock sharing, the strength-5 Reputation step, Reputation-
  range display access, folder-number surcharge, and delayed refill.
- **Association I/II:** worker escalation, all four task spaces, multiple
  different tasks, partner zoos, universities, asset rewards, donations, and
  support of existing projects. Zoo projects can be introduced and immediately
  supported from hand on either side, or from the Reputation-range display on
  side II after paying the folder-number surcharge.
- **Sponsors I/II:** Sponsor-level budgets, multiple cards on side II, printed
  prerequisites, unique-building placement, Reputation-range display access,
  folder-number surcharge, and the Break/money alternative.
- X tokens, Multiplier, Venom, Constriction, Determination extra actions, and
  Hypnosis use the shared action-session envelope and cannot leave a player in
  an action with no legal continuation.

The P4 action-domain suite contains 50 focused scenarios; the complete staged
official engine currently passes 103 Ark Nova official tests. Conservation
Project face compilation remains P5 work.
