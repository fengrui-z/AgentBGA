# P5 base Conservation Project status

Updated: 2026-08-25

P5 is complete for the 12 Base Conservation Projects and 20 Zoo Conservation
Projects. All 96 printed support tiers compile into executable requirements,
selector metadata, and rewards; the generated project-tier backlog is zero.

## Delivered

- Base projects count the seven distinct animal categories, five distinct
  continents, or the exact printed continent/category icon and threshold.
- Aquatic and Geological count both printed water/rock icons and water/rock
  requirements; Small Animals, Large Animals, and Research use the official
  size/icon thresholds.
- All five Breeding Programs require a matching Animal and a partner zoo from
  one of that Animal's continents. Their three globally exclusive support slots
  retain the exact 2/2, 1/2, and 2/0 Conservation/Reputation rewards.
- All ten Release into the Wild projects enumerate exact Animal and enclosure
  choices. Tier size must match exactly; special-enclosure tokens are removed
  first when possible, otherwise the smallest eligible occupied standard
  enclosure is emptied. The released card, its icons/tags, tucked cards, and
  printed Appeal leave the zoo while printed Conservation/Reputation and
  already-resolved abilities are not reversed.
- Introducing a Release project grants its one-time Reputation; later support
  does not. Migration Recording can support a Release face more than once but
  still cannot reuse an occupied tier in one Association action.
- The seven player-board project tokens are explicit state: four immediate-and-
  Break-income rewards (card, free size-2 enclosure, 5 money, 1 Conservation)
  and three immediate rewards (worker, 12 money, 3 X tokens). The player chooses
  both the token and whether its immediate reward resolves before or after the
  project reward.
- Two-player setup blocks high/middle/low tiers on the three base projects.
  Zoo-project display capacity follows player count; an evicted project's
  physical card enters the discard pile and its claim tokens return to their
  owners' general supplies, not their player boards.
- Unused Base Project cards gained through Assertion/Dominance can be introduced
  from hand above the Association board and are then treated as normal zoo
  projects.

Exhaustive family tests exercise every one of the 32 faces, with focused
scenarios for all reward/selector paths. P6 subsequently completed all 11 Final
Scoring cards and P8 connected them to complete-match scoring.
