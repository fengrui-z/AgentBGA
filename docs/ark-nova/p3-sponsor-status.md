# P3 base Sponsor status

Updated: 2026-08-25

P3 is complete for the 64 base-game Sponsor faces. All 77 transcribed effect
records and every printed prerequisite compile into the closed semantic/action
vocabulary; the generated Sponsor effect and prerequisite backlogs are both
zero. A Sponsor is playable only after its full record compiles.

## Delivered

- Immediate, triggered, passive, income, interaction, stored-token, and
  end-game effects use physical card instances and deterministic choices.
- Exact Appeal/Reputation thresholds, printed icons, direct rewards, tier
  tables, discounts, project hooks, and conditional scoring are represented.
- All fixed unique Sponsor buildings have mechanics-only polyhex footprints,
  rotations, terrain/border constraints, map rewards, and placement legality.
- Engineer, Hydrologist/Geologist, Diversity Researcher, Archeologist, Explorer,
  Migration Recording, both Breeding cards, WAZA programs, and Okapi Stable are
  connected to their action-domain windows rather than treated as text-only
  effects.
- WAZA Special Assignment searches by animal size, restricts later play, and
  awards the matching Appeal; WAZA Small Animals Program adds its paid extra
  play and optional display take; WAZA Large Animals Program places its free
  enclosure and ignores exactly one non-terrain prerequisite.
- Okapi Stable stores three physical tokens and opens a resumable optional
  Sponsor play after each herbivore-icon trigger, including unique-building
  placement and normal prerequisite/payment checks.

Scenario tests cover the reusable trigger streams, unique buildings, map and
project hooks, stored tokens, size-gated animal play, and corrected source
parameters. Action cards are complete in P4; remaining content work is in
Conservation Projects (P5) and Final Scoring (P6).
